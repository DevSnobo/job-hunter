Backend Engineer (Java) - Example Analytics GmbH

We are Example Analytics, a small product team in Berlin. We build a reporting
platform that our customers use every day, and we are looking for a backend
engineer who likes to own a feature from the first sketch to production.

Your tasks:

- Design and implement services in Java and Spring Boot.
- Model and query relational data; keep the schema honest and migrations boring.
- Write automated tests and keep the build fast and reproducible.
- Review code, improve our pipelines, and help the team ship small steps.
- Talk to support and customers to understand what the numbers mean.

What we look for:

- Solid Java and SQL experience, ideally with Spring Boot.
- Interest in clean architecture and in testing as a design tool.
- Docker and CI experience; Linux on the command line is normal for us.
- Good German and English, written and spoken.

What we offer:

- A permanent contract, 30 days of holiday, flexible hours.
- A small team where decisions are still short.
- A hardware budget of your choice.
